## Legal/Ethical Checklist

Before listing DaaS Portal on resume:

✅ **Built in personal time** (not during work hours)
✅ **Used personal equipment** (your laptop, not company resources)
✅ **No company IP used** (didn't copy FUTU-RE code/data)
✅ **No conflict of interest** (not competing with FUTU-RE business)
✅ **Can discuss publicly** (no confidential company information)
✅ **Original work** (you designed and coded it yourself)

If all checkboxes = YES → **Safe to list as personal project**
